# Drape Customer Order FAQ

Date: April 25, 2026

## What is the difference between ready-made and custom on Drape?

Ready-made means you are buying an existing item in a listed size.

Custom means the tailor is making the garment for your order, measurements, and requirements.

## When do I pay?

Ready-made:

- you pay at checkout

Custom:

- you pay after reviewing and accepting the tailor's quote

## How does delivery or shipping work?

For standard delivery and shipping, Drape manages the dispatch leg.

That means:

- you pay the standard fulfillment fee during checkout
- the tailor prepares the order
- Drape handles the normal dispatch process
- your order updates inside Drape as it moves forward

## How does pickup work?

If you choose pickup:

- your order stays inside Drape
- exact pickup details stay protected until the order is truly ready
- you should complete the handoff through the Drape collection flow

## Can I cancel my order?

Ready-made:

- if checkout is still `Payment pending`, you can cancel directly
- once the paid order is `Confirmed` or `Preparing order`, use the in-order cancellation review path
- once it is `Ready for Drape dispatch`, `Out for delivery`, `Shipped`, or `Ready for collection`, standard customer cancellation is closed
- after that point, use delivery review or support if something has gone wrong

Custom:

- if the order is still `Pending quote`, `Consultation`, or `Payment pending`, you can still walk away cleanly
- if a quote is already sitting with you, decline it there instead of trying to force a cancellation
- once the paid custom order is in `Confirmed`, `Designing`, or `Sourcing`, use Drape review to cancel fairly
- once cutting has started, standard cancellation is closed and any remedy becomes a Drape review or concern path instead

Refund logic:

- quote or item amount is easiest to refund before cutting or dispatch booking
- standard fulfillment fees are usually refundable before dispatch is booked
- paid consultation fees follow the consultation terms shown on the order
- rush or premium logistics fees are treated case by case

## What if my order is running late?

Keep the order thread updated in Drape and contact support if the delay becomes serious.

For custom orders, event timing matters. If the tailor sees the date is at risk, they should surface that early rather than going silent.

## What if I want to bring my own fabric?

That is allowed in some custom orders, but it has to be handled clearly.

Important things to understand:

- the tailor may inspect your fabric before agreeing to use it
- low-quality or insufficient fabric may be rejected
- production should not move forward until the tailor confirms fabric receipt

## What if the tailor wants to source fabric?

In that case, the fabric cost should be included in the quote before you pay.

The tailor should not quietly buy fabric first and then ask you for off-platform money later.

## What if the tailor says my fabric is not suitable?

The normal next steps are:

- send more fabric
- approve tailor-sourced replacement
- change the design
- or ask Drape to review the situation if the order cannot move forward fairly

## Are consultations free?

Some consultations can stay free.

If a tailor charges for consultation time:

- Drape shows the fee inside the order before the call starts
- the order also shows whether that fee is separate or credited toward the final quote
- the tailor cannot start the consultation call until the fee is confirmed
- the order shows the reschedule, no-show, and expiry rule before you pay

## What if I am ordering for my family or wedding group?

Those orders often need extra coordination. If you are ordering multiple custom garments for several people, expect Drape or the tailor to treat it as a more managed flow rather than a simple one-person order.

## What if delivery fails or the order is wrong?

Do not mark the order complete.

Keep the issue inside Drape so:

- the order history stays visible
- Drape can review the right evidence
- the next step can be handled fairly

## When is a ready-made exchange or refund normally allowed?

For launch, the working rule is:

- wrong item
- damaged item
- dispatch or delivery failure
- handoff evidence that does not match what really happened

Those are the cleanest exchange or refund cases.

Change-of-mind return is not automatic once preparation, dispatch, or collection has started.

## What if I cancel after paying for a consultation?

You can still stop the order before live production starts, but the consultation fee does not automatically behave like the main order money.

Instead:

- if the consultation was free, there is nothing extra to refund
- if the consultation fee was creditable, Drape can review whether it should be credited or refunded
- if the consultation fee was separate, it follows the no-show, expiry, and consultation terms already shown on the order

## What if I need to change pickup, delivery, or shipping after paying?

Use the in-order review path while the order is still pre-dispatch.

For launch:

- Drape can still review a fulfillment-method change before dispatch is actually booked
- after Drape has booked dispatch, it becomes an ops or support decision, not a self-serve edit
- recipient name, phone, or address can still be reviewed by ops if the parcel has not entered live dispatch yet

## What does delivered mean compared with complete?

`Delivered` means the handoff is recorded.

`Complete` means the order is fully closed.

That gap matters because you still have a short window after delivery or collection to:

- confirm everything is right
- review the order
- or raise a concern before the order closes cleanly

## What if I need to change delivery or shipping after paying?

Do not arrange it privately.

If handoff has not started yet, use the in-order cancellation or change review path so Drape can decide whether the order should:

- switch method cleanly
- be cancelled and refunded
- or go through support review before dispatch
